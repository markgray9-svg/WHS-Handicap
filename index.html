All figures use the published WHS formula: Score Differential = (Constant ÷ Slope Rating) × (Gross Score − Course Rating − PCC). The Handicap Index averages the lowest scoring differentials from the most recent 20 rounds per the official WHS table, rounded to the nearest tenth. Rounds marked Custom Course use their own Course Rating and Slope Rating (with the selected tee's Constant, or 113 if no tee is chosen). Setup data and round history are saved automatically in this browser.
